Summary: "Attendance & Salary" is designed with user privacy as our top priority. All your attendance entries, duty shifts, overtime hours, and salary calculations are stored strictly locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your personal work records on any external server.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Attendance & Salary ("we", "our", or "the app"), with package name com.conicapps.attendance, handles your information when you use our Android mobile application.

2. Information We Store Locally on Your Device

The app allows you to record duty, attendance, and salary parameters to compute your monthly wages and overtime. The following data is processed and stored locally in your phone's private internal storage (Room Database):

  • Employee Profile: Name, Employee ID, and Department/Designation (entered by you optionally).
  • Attendance & Duty Logs: Date-wise attendance status (Present, Half Day, Absent, Leave/Holiday, Sunday Duty).
  • Shift Timings: Clock-in and clock-out hours, break deductions (lunch and tea breaks).
  • Salary & Overtime Settings: Base salary rate, 26-day overtime divisor preference, and overtime multipliers (1x or 2x).

Important: None of this financial or personal duty information is sent to our servers or any third-party cloud database.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

To keep the application free to use, we display non-intrusive banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

Google AdMob may collect and process certain technical diagnostic and telemetry information strictly in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. This may include:

  • Anonymous device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID, subject to your Android device settings).
  • IP address and approximate coarse location (for regional ad relevance).
  • Ad interaction data and technical crash/diagnostic metrics.

For more details on how Google uses data from apps that integrate its SDKs, please visit: Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Technologies.

4. Permissions Used by the App

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required strictly to load AdMob banner advertisements and verify network connectivity for ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK to check whether an active internet connection is available before requesting an ad.

The app does NOT request or access sensitive device permissions such as Camera, Contacts, Microphone, SMS, or precise GPS location.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your attendance and salary logs are stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete or modify any specific date's duty log at any time using the Delete button in the duty sheet.
  • You can reset all settings to default values using the "Reset to Defaults" option in the Settings screen.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing the app storage from your Android device settings will permanently erase all stored attendance and salary records.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and will be available inside the app settings.
